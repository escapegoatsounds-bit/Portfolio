Abdelaziz Askar · Head of Social & AI · Tonic International · Dubai, UAE · 18 years in the industry

Born in Kuwait, raised in Alexandria. I've spent 18 years working on brands from Cairo to the Gulf — TVCs, campaigns, social, strategy, and whatever else the job needed that week.
